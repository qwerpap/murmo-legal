Murmo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Murmo is a sleep sounds mixer. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads.

What stays on your iPhone

Your saved mixes and your last mix are stored only on your device. All sounds are bundled with the app or generated on the device. Your mixes never leave your iPhone and we cannot see them.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store; we receive no payment details. We use Adapty to confirm your subscription and to count paywall views and purchases. Adapty receives an anonymous app profile ID, purchase events, the onboarding step you reached and your quiz answers (sleep goal, what keeps you awake, sounds you like), so we can see which paywall works for which listener. It never receives your name, your email or your mixes. We don't use advertising, tracking or other analytics SDKs, and we don't collect IDFA.

Data we collect

Only the anonymous purchase data described above. Murmo does not sell personal data and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Murmo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored.
