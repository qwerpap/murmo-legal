Murmo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Murmo is a sleep sounds mixer. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no third-party analytics.

What stays on your iPhone

Your quiz answers (goal, what keeps you awake, sounds you like), your saved mixes and your last mix are stored only on your device. All sounds are bundled with the app or generated on the device. None of this data leaves your iPhone and we cannot see it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Data we collect

None. Murmo does not collect, transmit or sell personal data and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Murmo does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored.
