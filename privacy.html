Privacy

Updated October 6, 2026

Murmo plays sound while you fall asleep. It has no account, no sign-in and no ads, and almost nothing about you leaves the phone.

On your iPhone

Your saved mixes, your last mix, the volume of every layer, the sleep timer and your settings stay in the app. All sounds are bundled with Murmo or generated on the device as you listen, so playing a mix needs no internet.

Sound and the lock screen

Murmo uses the audio system so the mix keeps playing with the screen locked, and shows play and pause on the lock screen and in Control Center. It does not record audio and never asks for the microphone.

What is shared, and with whom

Murmo uses Adapty to show the right paywall and to confirm that Murmo Pro is active. Adapty receives a random profile ID, your purchase events and status from the App Store, the paywall that was shown, the start-up step you reached, and your answers to the start-up quiz (the sleep goal you picked, what keeps you awake, the sounds you like). That tells us which paywall suits which kind of listener. It does not receive your name, your email or your mixes.

Apple processes payments; we never see card details. There are no advertising SDKs, no tracking across other apps or sites and no IDFA.

Writing to us

Settings › Contact support opens your mail app with a draft that carries the profile ID so we can find your subscription. It is sent only if you send it.

Deleting

Delete the app to remove saved mixes and settings from the phone. To remove the anonymous purchase profile as well, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com with your profile ID.

Sound credits

Some recordings are used under open licenses (CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY 3.0, CC0, public domain); the full list is in Settings › Sound credits.

Children, changes

Murmo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. A changed policy is posted here with a new date.
