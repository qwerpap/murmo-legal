Murmo — Support

Questions, a sound you miss, or a problem with your subscription? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — we answer within two working days.

Restore a purchase

Open Murmo → Settings (gear icon) → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancel a subscription

Settings app on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Murmo.

Sound stops when the screen locks

Make sure Silent mode or Focus doesn't mute media, and that no other app starts playing audio.
