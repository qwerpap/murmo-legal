Sound stops when the screen locks

Check that Silent mode or a Focus is not muting media and that no other app has started playing audio.

The mix feels too quiet or too loud

Each layer has its own slider; lower the loudest layer first, then adjust the phone volume.

Where did my saved mixes go?

Mixes live only on the iPhone where you saved them; there is no account to sync them from.
